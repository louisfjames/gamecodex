What is GameCodex?

GameCodex is a personal game tracking platform built for players who want to make sense of their gaming library. Whether you're juggling multiple games across different platforms or trying to finally clear your backlog, GameCodex gives you a simple, organised space to log what you're playing, what you've completed, and what's next.

Powered by the IGDB database, GameCodex lets you search thousands of games, pick your platform, and add them straight to your library with cover art included. Track your start and completion dates, filter by status, and get a clear picture of your gaming history - all in one place.

Free to use. No clutter. Just your games.
