Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about GameCodex.

GameCodex is a personal game tracking platform that lets you organise your gaming library across multiple platforms. Search for games, add them to your profile, and track your progress with statuses like Currently Playing, Completed, Backlog, and Abandoned.

Use the Search page to find your game, select the correct platform, choose a status, and click Add Entry to Profile. The game will then appear in your library with its cover art.

No – the platform is locked when you first add the entry. If you need to change it, delete the entry and add it again with the correct platform. This keeps your library accurate and prevents accidental changes.

GameCodex is powered by the IGDB (Internet Game Database) API, which provides game titles, cover art, platform information, and descriptions for thousands of games.

Currently Playing – games you're actively playing right now.
Completed – games you've finished.
Backlog – games you own or want to play but haven't started yet.
Abandoned – games you've stopped playing and don't plan to return to.
